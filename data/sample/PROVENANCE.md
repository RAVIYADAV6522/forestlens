## Rules

- Verify the licence for the exact asset, not just the dataset's front page, and paste the
  terms that apply.
- A consumer map screenshot is not measurement data. Do not use one for the final submission.
- If the GSD comes from the raster transform, note the CRS. If it is assumed, say "assumed"
  and give the reasoning — the app records which of the two it used in every export.
